Home / Platform

From meter to registry, on one platform.

Watraq is the data backbone of a water credit project. It collects every reading, checks it, stores the evidence, and produces the reports verifiers and regulators ask for.

Overview

Digital MRV, purpose-built for water

Most water data sits in logbooks, spreadsheets and SCADA screens that nobody outside the plant can see. Watraq brings it together in one place, cleans it, and links each number to its evidence, so a verifier can trace any credit back to the meter reading behind it.

  • Works with existing meters, SCADA and manual logs
  • Multi-site, multi-project, role-based access
  • Mobile app for field data and geotagged photos
  • Exports to PDF, Excel and registry templates

Modules

Everything a water project needs

Data capture

IoT gateways for electromagnetic and ultrasonic flow meters, level sensors and quality analysers (pH, TSS, BOD, COD). Works offline and syncs later.

Water balance engine

Daily balance per site and zone. Flags meter drift, gaps and imbalances automatically.

Evidence vault

Lab reports, calibration certificates, photos and invoices, each time-stamped and hash-linked into a tamper-evident log.

Methodology mapping

Baseline, project and leakage calculations set up for the programme or standard you use.

Monitoring reports

Monitoring reports and verification packs generated from live data in a few clicks.

Credit ledger

Estimated, verified, issued, transferred and retired credits, all tracked with serials.

ESG & BRSR

Water withdrawal, consumption, discharge and intensity metrics, ready for disclosure.

GIS view

Map every asset: STPs, borewells, ponds, recharge pits and farm plots.

Group projects

Combine hundreds of farms or buildings into one project, with fair benefit-sharing.

Data integrity

Built so verifiers can trust it

Source-tagged readings

Every value is tagged with where it came from (IoT, SCADA, manual or lab) and how confident we are in it.

Immutable audit trail

Edits never overwrite. Corrections are logged with who made them, when and why.

No double counting

A litre claimed once can't be claimed again by another project, buyer or report.
