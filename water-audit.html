Home / Water Audit

Water audit reports that lead somewhere.

A good water audit does more than satisfy a regulator. It shows where every litre goes and which fixes pay back fastest, and it sets the baseline for future water credits.

Audit types

An audit for every kind of site

Industrial

Process-wise balance, specific water consumption, ETP/STP and recycling, for CGWA and pollution-board needs.

Municipal

System input vs billed volume, NRW by zone, bulk metering, STP reuse and supply equity.

Building & campus

Fixtures, HVAC, landscaping, STP reuse and rainwater harvesting for offices, hotels, hospitals and townships.

Agricultural

Irrigation efficiency, groundwater use, crop water need and recharge potential for farms and FPOs.

Methodology

How we audit

1

Scoping

Objectives, boundaries, data request and site plan.

2

Data review

Bills, meter logs, consents, borewell records, production data.

3

Site walk-through

Every source, user, tank, line and outfall mapped and photographed.

4

Measurement

Portable ultrasonic flow meters and sampling where meters are missing.

5

Water balance

Reconciled balance with unaccounted water quantified.

6

Benchmarking

Specific water consumption compared against sector norms.

7

Opportunities

Savings measures sized with capex, opex and payback.

8

Report & roadmap

Final report, presentation to management, credit readiness.

    Deliverables

    What's in your water audit report

    A clear report, an editable data file and a management presentation. Everything is loaded into Watraq so you can track progress afterwards.

    • Water balance diagram (Sankey) from source to discharge
    • Metering adequacy and calibration status, with a meter plan
    • Water quality summary for raw, treated and recycled water
    • Rainwater harvesting and recharge potential
    • Regulatory compliance checklist (CGWA, SPCB consent, BRSR)
    • Action plan with capex, annual savings, payback and water credit potential
