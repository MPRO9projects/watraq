Watraq is built by M Pro9 Private Limited, an environmental technology and consulting company that turns sustainability activity into trustworthy data.

Our mission

Make every saved litre count, and pay off

Water projects in India often stall for a simple reason: the benefits are real but invisible. Nobody measures them well enough to pay for them. Watraq exists to fix that.

We combine engineering water audits, practical project design and a digital MRV platform so that municipalities, industries, organisations and farmers can prove their water impact and be rewarded for it.

  • Honest: we tell you early if a project won't qualify
  • Evidence-first: metered data over assumptions
  • Inclusive: farmers and small towns deserve access to credit markets too
