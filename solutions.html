A water credit playbook for every sector.

Municipalities, industry, organisations and farmers save water in very different ways. Here's what Watraq does for each.

Municipalities & ULBs

Turn sewage into a revenue stream

Cities treat millions of litres every day and most of it is discharged unused. Reusing it and fixing losses creates a large, measurable water benefit.

STPreuse to industry
NRWloss reduction
Lakesrejuvenation

What we do

  • City water audit: system input, billed consumption, NRW, and water balance by zone (DMA)
  • Treated water reuse plans: sell STP output to industry, thermal plants, construction and parks
  • Smart metering at STP outlets and bulk supply points with live monitoring
  • Leak detection and pressure management programmes, with the savings documented
  • Lake and tank rejuvenation, with storage and recharge benefits quantified
  • Water credit project documentation, so credit revenue can help fund O&M

Aligned with

AMRUT 2.0National Framework on Safe Reuse of Treated WaterGreen Credit ProgrammeJal Shakti Abhiyan

Industry

Recycle more, withdraw less, prove it

Textiles, pharma, food & beverage, chemicals, steel and power all depend on water. Recycling effluent cuts cost and risk.

ETPrecycling
ZLDreadiness
SWCper product

What we do

  • Industrial water audit with process-wise water balance and specific water consumption
  • ETP / STP performance review and recycling options (UF, RO, MEE)
  • Cooling tower, boiler and process efficiency measures
  • Groundwater compliance: metering, telemetry and the audit reports large users need under CGWA guidelines
  • Use of municipal treated water in place of freshwater, with contracts and metering
  • Water credit generation and, where needed, buying credits to offset remaining use

Aligned with

CGWA guidelinesCPCB / SPCB consent normsZLDISO 46001BRSR Principle 6

Organisations

Water neutral. Water positive. Reported properly.

Corporates, IT parks, universities, hospitals, hotels and residential townships can make credible water commitments.

BRSRdisclosure
CSRwater projects
Netwater positive

What we do

  • Campus and building water audits, including STP reuse for flushing, landscaping and HVAC
  • Rainwater harvesting and recharge design with metered evidence
  • Water footprint and water-neutrality roadmaps
  • CSR watershed and community water projects with verified outcomes
  • BRSR water indicators: withdrawal, consumption, discharge, intensity, generated from Watraq data
  • Buying credits from verified projects to cover what you can't reduce on-site

Farmers & FPOs

More crop per drop, and a new income

Agriculture uses most of India's water. Small changes on many farms add up, and grouping farms makes them creditable.

Drip& sprinkler
Ponds& recharge
FPOaggregation

What we do

  • Group projects through FPOs, cooperatives and NGOs so smallholders can take part
  • Micro-irrigation (drip, sprinkler) and soil-moisture-based scheduling
  • Farm ponds, check dams and recharge structures, with storage measured
  • Safe use of treated wastewater for irrigation near cities
  • Simple mobile data capture with geotagged photos, in local languages
  • Transparent benefit-sharing so credit income reaches the farmer

Aligned with

PMKSY: Per Drop More CropAtal Bhujal YojanaGreen Credit Programme
