FAQ

Common questions

What is a water credit?

A water credit is a tradable or reportable certificate representing a verified volume of water that has been saved, recycled, recharged or restored compared to a baseline. Credits are usually denominated in volume (for example cubic metres) and are only issued after independent verification.

Who can earn water credits?

Any entity that creates a measurable, additional water benefit: municipalities reusing treated sewage or cutting losses, industries recycling effluent, campuses and real-estate projects harvesting rainwater, and farmers adopting micro-irrigation or recharge, usually grouped through an FPO.

Who buys water credits?

Mostly companies with water-stewardship, water-neutrality or ESG goals, especially water-intensive sectors such as beverages, textiles, data centres, pharma and automotive. Some programmes also let credits be used against compliance obligations. Demand and prices depend on the programme.

Does Watraq issue credits itself?

No. Watraq does the measurement, data, documentation and verification support. Credits are issued by the relevant programme or registry after independent validation and verification. We never promise issuance or a price.

What does a water audit report include?

A water balance from source to discharge, metering assessment, specific water consumption, losses and leakage, STP/ETP performance, recycling and rainwater harvesting potential, regulatory compliance, and a costed list of actions with payback periods.
